# Per-output failure sustain

Per-output sustain lets a source require repeated fresh measurements across a duration before a WARN or CRIT can affect incidents. The first consumer use case is a five-minute grace period for transient link loss. An output still reports its measured result at the normal cadence. The controller separately qualifies the severity used for incidents, acknowledgements and delivery.

## Policy and compatibility

Add a source-owned integer `sustain_seconds` to the SDK output declaration, from 0 through 86,400 inclusive. Omission and zero are equivalent. The SDK omits zero from canonical metadata so the existing output representation and definition fingerprints stay stable. Validate raw values at authoring, custom inventory, and controller admission before generic numeric normalization. Canonicalize zero before hashing or comparing inventory payloads, including replays. Compatibility assumes an identical enclosing source revision and, for externally supplied metadata, identical opaque supplied revision identifiers. Positive values participate in the existing definition fingerprint and authorized generation. Keep the setting out of operator parameter rules, result reports, discovery scope declarations and configuration Git settings. Read the policy from the report's admitted configuration, since displayed stream metadata can already describe a desired generation. Snapshot the freshness limit in configurations that enable sustain. It supplies the cutoff for sustain even when a new desired generation changes the assignment's freshness before cutover. The raw projection and its existing freshness classification remain separate from that qualification cutoff. Upgrade both controller and SDK before publishing positive sustain values.

Zero uses the existing incident reducer behavior. Enabling sustain on a continuous stream preserves previously confirmed and already effective health and open incidents. Seed the initial effective value from the stream's confirmed evidence before applying the new report. A fresh result applies the new policy prospectively. Disabling sustain with a candidate pending cannot make that raw result effective. Keep the prior qualified view through a cutover or an unavailable result and return to the default path on the first fresh authorized measurement. A policy, generation or session change discards unqualified continuity, while prior qualified health can remain visible with its original evidence until fresh evidence supersedes it. Stream replacement gets a clean projection. Explicit existing retirement and configuration-recovery rules still apply.

## Qualification

Measure continuity separately at WARN-or-worse and CRIT. Each candidate has a start observation, original source timestamp and controller receipt timestamp. While either candidate exists, keep the latest advancing failure sample and receipt. A fresh WARN preserves WARN continuity and clears the CRIT candidate. A fresh CRIT can continue both. An OK clears both and immediately becomes effective. A fresh WARN downgrades already qualified CRIT to WARN immediately. A controller restart resumes the stored candidate using the same checks as before restart.

- A new report can advance a candidate only after ordinary admission, with a present non-UNKNOWN result for that output, a known original source time, and a fresh measurement. Valid siblings advance independently in partial reports. An omitted output, UNKNOWN, stale evidence or a scope change clears pending candidates without recovering or replacing qualified evidence.
- Store the previous sample's absolute expiry using its original source timestamp plus the admitted generation's freshness limit. Receipt must precede that expiry. Equality breaks continuity. The original source time must strictly advance. A regressed receipt time breaks continuity. After a break, the current fresh failure can seed a new candidate, and that sample alone supplies zero elapsed duration.
- Qualification needs both the original source span and the controller receipt span to reach the configured duration. The crossing is inclusive, using microsecond comparisons. Historical and duplicate reports leave candidates untouched. Sweeps and elapsed wall time can invalidate a pending candidate but can never qualify one.
- A higher reported severity below its duration leaves prior qualified state and evidence in place. A WARN threshold crossing during reported CRIT can establish qualified WARN. A qualified result at the reported severity can update impacts immediately under existing rules. If CRIT is pending over already qualified WARN, preserve the previously qualified contribution and its impacts until a threshold crosses or a fresh WARN is reported. Keep the supporting observation's raw verdict available when it differs from the effective severity.

## Persistence and read paths

Store bounded qualification alongside each stream's current projection in PostgreSQL. Include the policy, candidate generation/session, start and latest observation identities/times, and independently attributed qualified severity/evidence. The observation and existing confirmed measurement projection remain raw and immutable in meaning, so monitoring loss, freshness, replay, and the assignment's measured generation continue using original evidence. A legacy stream has no qualification state and keeps its existing behavior. The stream row and its observation, incident state and notification jobs change under the existing ingestion transaction and lock order.

Compute qualification after the existing per-output measurement projection. Call the ordinary or grouped incident reducer only for a fresh result that supplies effective evidence. Pass the effective verdict to the reducer and the original observation as evidence. A pending or unavailable result cannot update incident provenance, add impacts, end an acknowledgement, or change a contributor's grouped facts. The same qualified transition continues to drive existing correlation and delivery logic.

Pending display checks the current authorization, session, stream and assignment retirement, and stored freshness cutoff. Retention preserves the endpoints of stored candidates until they are cleared. The next admitted report can clear invalid candidates. Retention also clears invalid or expired candidates in bounded batches before the age and count pruning passes, under the normal assignment lock with a post-lock validity check. Acquire those assignment locks in the existing sorted assignment set before condition and monitoring-loss locks. Clearing before unprotecting evidence makes expiry irreversible even if the controller clock later regresses. The current qualified observation uses the existing confirmed measurement's stream history lifetime. Keeping endpoints does not guarantee retention of all intermediate samples.

Show effective severity/evidence and active pending severity separately from raw measurement and collection status in shared result detail. Historical timeline rows keep reporting the raw result and can point to exact retained incident evidence. Current qualification does not imply reconstructed historical timer state. Measurement-based result filters keep their raw semantics, and incident counts continue to derive from incidents. Resolve pending validity against the authoritative assignment and runner, and expose a typed optional projection in result inspection. An output with a pending first failure has no qualified health. Existing consumers of confirmed measurement and source freshness keep their current semantics.

## Verification

Use shared Python and Elixir vectors for policy bounds and default compatibility. Define fixture sequences with exact source and receipt times, stopped timers, expected qualified evidence and impact behavior before wiring the reducer. Exercise controlled PostgreSQL ingestion around exact duration and freshness equality, advancing versus regressed source or receipt time, fast backlog, absent or unknown sibling output, fenced and duplicate reports, generation and session handovers, and recovery. Verify qualified WARN and CRIT independently, pending escalation with acknowledgement, immediate impact changes after qualification, grouped conditions, zero duration, and both retention limits, and expiry cleanup followed by a clock rollback. Recreate the controller during a real installed-runner scenario to verify continuity survives the process restart and drives delivery only to a test receiver. Verify the shared result detail in both routes, including a raw CRIT with a pending or lower effective severity.
